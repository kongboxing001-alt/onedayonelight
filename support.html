Support

One verse a day, in two languages, for the whole family to read together.

Restore Pro on a new device

Open Settings and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Family Sharing

Pro is shared with your family through Apple Family Sharing. Family members can tap “Restore purchases” to turn it on.

Saved verses on another device

Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices. Syncing may take a few minutes.

Adding the widget

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or +, and search for “One Light”.

The daily reminder does not appear

Check that notifications are allowed for the app in the iPhone or iPad Settings app, then set the time again under Settings → Daily reminder.

Bible translations

Every translation in the app is in the public domain or freely licensed. The full list is under Settings → More → Translations.
