Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

One Day, One Light does not collect any data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

On your device

Your settings, saved verses and reading history are stored on your device. The app does not send them to us or to anyone else.

iCloud

If you use iCloud, your saved verses and some settings (languages, moods, holidays) are kept in sync through your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud storage. We cannot see this information.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Sharing posters

When you share or save a verse poster, the system share sheet is used. The app never reads your photo library.

Purchases

Pro and gifts of support are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
